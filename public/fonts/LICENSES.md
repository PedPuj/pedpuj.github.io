# Typefaces

All of them are served from this site rather than from a CDN, so that nothing
on the critical path of a page load lives on someone else's domain.

The first two are the site's own voice. The last three exist for one thing
only — the napkin on the About page — and are loaded by that page alone.

## Switzer — `switzer-400.woff2`, `switzer-500.woff2`

Indian Type Foundry, via Fontshare. Free for personal and commercial use under
the ITF Free Font Licence, which permits self-hosting.
<https://www.fontshare.com/fonts/switzer>

## IBM Plex Mono — `plex-mono-400-latin.woff2`, `plex-mono-400-latin-ext.woff2`

IBM. SIL Open Font Licence 1.1.
<https://github.com/IBM/plex/blob/master/LICENSE.txt>

Subset to Latin and Latin Extended, as served by Google Fonts.

## Oswald — `oswald-var-latin.woff2`

Vernon Adams, Kalapi Gajjar, Cyreal. SIL Open Font Licence 1.1.
<https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Oswald>

A variable file, so weights 500 and 700 come out of the same 21 KB.
Subset to Latin, as served by Google Fonts. Used only on the About page,
for the napkin's name and its lists.

## Tinos — `tinos-700-latin.woff2`

Steve Matteson, Ascender. Apache Licence 2.0.
<https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Tinos>

Metric-compatible with Times New Roman, which is what a Spanish jobbing
printer would have set the napkin's small print in. Bold only, Latin only,
About page only.

## Yellowtail — `yellowtail-400-latin.woff2`

Astigmatic. SIL Open Font Licence 1.1.
<https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Yellowtail>

One word on the whole site is set in it: the town at the foot of the napkin.
